2 · CRANK TORQUE OVER ONE TURN (all 8 legs, at the crankshafts' common speed)
  Geometry: sim/toy_leg_3d_rows_cad.json [0]: crank r 3.91 mm; stride 9.65 mm per half turn, bob 4.56 mm, step 9.5 mm, feet slip 6.5 mm/leg/turn
  Foot vertical speed per crank radian: up to 7.4 mm/rad (crank pin 3.91 mm/rad: the leg multiplies it 1.9 ×)
  Plain estimate: W × bob × 2 steps per turn = 4.54 × 4.56 × 2 = 41 N·mm of lift work per turn → mean 6.6 N·mm, before peaks and friction
  Assumptions: support feet within 2.0 mm of the lowest; μ joints PLA/PLA 0.35, screw/PLA 0.25; ground μ (0.4, 0.6); centre of mass ±5.0 mm; each crankshaft's net torque counted only when positive
   even split, μ_ground 0.4: peak  44.7 N·mm at  11.0° (lift 22.2 + ground slip 8.1 + joints 14.4 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 16.9; no leg helping another: peak 45.0, mean 17.4
   even split, μ_ground 0.6: peak  51.0 N·mm at  11.0° (lift 22.2 + ground slip 12.1 + joints 16.7 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 20.3; no leg helping another: peak 51.4, mean 20.8
  worst split, μ_ground 0.4: peak  48.9 N·mm at 358.5° (lift 23.5 + ground slip 9.7 + joints 15.7 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 21.1; no leg helping another: peak 49.5, mean 21.4
  worst split, μ_ground 0.6: peak  56.6 N·mm at 358.5° (lift 23.5 + ground slip 14.5 + joints 18.5 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 25.4; no leg helping another: peak 57.2, mean 25.8

3 · TARGET CRANK SPEED
  Stride 9.65 mm per half turn → 19.3 mm per crank turn
  2 cm/s →   62 rpm at the crank
  3 cm/s →   93 rpm at the crank
  5 cm/s →  155 rpm at the crank
  Range for a desk toy this size: 2-5 cm/s; aim ~3 cm/s at 4.8 V, not under ~1.5 cm/s at 4.0 V
