SPIDER-BOT · N20 GEAR MOTOR SIZING  (sim/motor_sizing.py)

1 · ROBOT MASS
  Design mass given with --mass: 457 g (cad/chassis/mass_estimate.py: CAD masses, contingency by source included)  → weight W = 4.48 N

2 · CRANK TORQUE OVER ONE TURN (all 8 legs, at the crankshafts' common speed)
  Geometry: sim/toy_leg_3d_rows_cad.json [0]: crank r 3.91 mm; stride 9.65 mm per half turn, bob 4.56 mm, step 9.5 mm, feet slip 6.5 mm/leg/turn
  Foot vertical speed per crank radian: up to 7.4 mm/rad (crank pin 3.91 mm/rad: the leg multiplies it 1.9 ×)
  Plain estimate: W × bob × 2 steps per turn = 4.48 × 4.56 × 2 = 41 N·mm of lift work per turn → mean 6.5 N·mm, before peaks and friction
  Assumptions: support feet within 2.0 mm of the lowest; μ joints PLA/PLA 0.35, screw/PLA 0.25; ground μ (0.4, 0.6); centre of mass ±5.0 mm; each crankshaft's net torque counted only when positive
   even split, μ_ground 0.4: peak  44.1 N·mm at  11.0° (lift 21.9 + ground slip 8.0 + joints 14.2 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 16.7; no leg helping another: peak 44.4, mean 17.2
   even split, μ_ground 0.6: peak  50.3 N·mm at  11.0° (lift 21.9 + ground slip 12.0 + joints 16.5 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 20.1; no leg helping another: peak 50.7, mean 20.6
  worst split, μ_ground 0.4: peak  48.2 N·mm at 358.5° (lift 23.2 + ground slip 9.6 + joints 15.5 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 20.8; no leg helping another: peak 48.9, mean 21.2
  worst split, μ_ground 0.6: peak  55.8 N·mm at 178.5° (lift 23.2 + ground slip 14.3 + joints 18.3 + legs' weight 0.0), mean 25.1; no leg helping another: peak 56.5, mean 25.4
  → Worst crank torque T_c = 56 N·mm = 0.57 kg·cm (a 14.3 N push on the 3.91 mm crank); typical peak 44 N·mm
  → Mean crank torque 17 (even split, μ 0.4) to 25 N·mm (worst split, μ 0.6)

3 · TARGET CRANK SPEED
  Stride 9.65 mm per half turn → 19.3 mm per crank turn
  2 cm/s →   62 rpm at the crank
  3 cm/s →   93 rpm at the crank
  5 cm/s →  155 rpm at the crank
  Range for a desk toy this size: 2-5 cm/s; aim ~3 cm/s at 4.8 V, not under ~1.5 cm/s at 4.0 V
